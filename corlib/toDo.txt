Comment: it appears more natural to have the orders as template parameters
         instead of node/point numbers. Reasons:
         - As a user I would not care too much how many nodes a cubic
           tetrahedron really has.
         - The number of nodes of the simplex quadrature rules is rather
           mysterious and needs to be looked up in the implementation.
         - Look at ShapefunTraits in order to get a taste how complicated
           type derivations based on the number of nodes are!
